EGO POWER+

Helping the number-one rated brand in cordless outdoor power prove that battery can beat gas: national campaigns, EGO Labs 2.0 and the launch of EGO Commercial.

Year
Realm Agency
Type
Account Management

At Realm Agency, Taylor led the agency-side relationship with EGO POWER+, supporting brand growth, product launches and integrated marketing for one of the outdoor power equipment industry’s leading innovators.

The work spanned national advertising, the evolution of EGO Labs 2.0, and the development and launch of EGO Commercial, supporting the introduction of 100+ products and EGO’s market-share growth.

Taylor’s role.

Taylor led agency-side strategy, client relationships, campaign development and end-to-end execution across EGO’s consumer and commercial portfolios, with brand leadership, creative teams, production partners and outside stakeholders.

Her remit covered brand positioning, product launches, creative direction, influencer marketing, sponsorship activations and integrated campaigns, with hands-on oversight of pre-production, production and post. The work ran across national TV, digital and social, branded content, product photo and video, web, retail merchandising, in-store displays, packaging and e-commerce, including Amazon, Lowe’s and Ace, on multimillion-dollar production budgets.

Realm Agency.

Brand strategy · Integrated marketing · Product launches · Brand development · Creative production

The strategy.

EGO was challenging an industry long dominated by gas, proving battery technology could match or beat it without the noise, fumes or maintenance. The strategy: change perceptions, demonstrate the technology and accelerate adoption at home and on the job.

Building on EGO’s reputation for unconventional demonstrations, from loading a string trimmer while skydiving to running a snow blower in Antarctica, EGO Labs 2.0 pushed product storytelling further. One standout put the EGO Z6 zero-turn mower on Chicago’s Soldier Field to show its power and runtime on one of football’s most famous surfaces.

EGO Commercial.

Realm also helped develop and launch EGO Commercial, a distinct brand identity and marketing foundation positioning EGO’s professional-grade equipment as a real alternative to gas.

The machine.

EGO rates the Z6 at the power of a 22 HP gas engine, with a top speed of 8 mph and up to two acres on a charge from four 10Ah batteries, with room for six. Four brushless motors, three drive modes, 32 LEDs and Bluetooth, with a five-year warranty on the machine and batteries.

Impact.

The work contributed to EGO’s continued market-share growth and its recognition as the number-one rated brand in cordless outdoor power equipment.

Beyond single campaigns, it set up a scalable approach connecting product innovation, storytelling and omnichannel execution, supporting EGO’s growing ecosystem and its move into the professional market.
