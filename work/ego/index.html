EGO Z6 Launch

Launch creative for the EGO Z6, the world’s first platform-compatible zero-turn rider.

Year
2021
Type
Production

EGO introduced the 42-inch Z6 as the world’s first platform-compatible zero-turn rider: it runs on the same 56V batteries as the rest of the POWER+ line. It reached Lowe’s, Ace Hardware and EGO dealers from February 2021, after what EGO says was more than six years of research and development.

Its launch film, “Power without limits,” published in March 2021, carried the line “Power Like No Other.”

Taylor’s role.

At Realm Agency, Taylor oversaw the delivery of more than 20,000 creative and campaign assets for EGO, SKIL and FLEX, supporting over 200 product launches, and executive produced campaigns across live action, VFX, animation and post-production.

The machine.

EGO rates the Z6 at the power of a 22 HP gas engine, with a top speed of 8 mph and up to two acres on a charge from four 10Ah batteries, with room for six. Four brushless motors, three drive modes, 32 LEDs and Bluetooth, with a five-year warranty on the machine and batteries.
