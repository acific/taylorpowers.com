EGO Z6 Launch

Launch creative for EGO’s Z6, the world’s first platform-compatible zero-turn rider.

Year
2024 — 2025
Type
Production

The 42-inch Z6 zero-turn riding mower (ZT4204L) runs on four of EGO’s 56V batteries, the same packs that power the rest of the POWER+ line, and cuts up to two acres on a charge. The launch film, “Power without limits,” introduced it as the world’s first platform-compatible rider.

At Realm Agency, Taylor oversaw the delivery of more than 20,000 creative and campaign assets for EGO, SKIL and FLEX, supporting over 200 product launches, and executive produced campaigns across live action, VFX, animation and post-production.
