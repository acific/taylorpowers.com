SuperFile × TKO (UFC & WWE)

A global partnership with TKO Group Holdings that made SuperFile the first Official Secure File Sharing Partner of both UFC and WWE.

Year
2026
Type
Brand Management

As VP of Brand at SuperFile, Taylor led the brand-side strategy and activation of a global partnership with TKO Group Holdings, connecting SuperFile with two of the world’s most recognised sports and entertainment properties: UFC and WWE.

The multi-year agreement created a platform across marquee UFC events, Fight Nights and WWE Premium Live Events, combining broadcast exposure, digital content, in-venue branding, experiential activations and executive hospitality.

Taylor’s role.

Taylor led SuperFile’s brand-side strategy, planning and activation across UFC and WWE: sponsorship integration, marketing, creative direction, branded content and hands-on production.

That included broadcast and in-venue branding, digital integrations, event activations, VIP and executive hospitality, investor and partner experiences, merchandise, gifting, food and beverage, and coordination with TKO’s partnership, marketing and production teams, including UFC’s Super Replay and Keys to Victory features and the SuperFile-presented UFC FREEDOM 250 Post-Show.

VP of Brand, SuperFile.

Global partnership · Live events · Broadcast integration

The strategy.

Ahead of a broader market launch, SuperFile’s priority was to accelerate awareness, establish credibility and create business opportunities through established global partners.

TKO offered two distinct, highly engaged audiences through one relationship. The approach went beyond exposure: integrating SuperFile into major live events, broadcast programming and premium experiences, and using TKO’s network for executive relationships, investor engagement and business development.

The White House.

Five days later SuperFile was an Official Partner of UFC FREEDOM 250 on the White House grounds, with its branding inside the Octagon and broadcast integrations reaching an estimated one billion households in 210 countries and territories.

SuperFile presented the night’s Super Replay of its most memorable moments, a “Keys to Victory” feature on each fighter’s strategy, and the one-hour UFC FREEDOM 250 SuperFile Post-Show. Its mark also stood in front of the scale at the weigh-ins.

UFC 329.

SuperFile was then presenting partner of UFC 329, McGregor vs. Holloway 2, at T-Mobile Arena in Las Vegas on July 11, the headline event of International Fight Week, with activation across UFC’s numbered events and Fight Nights.

Its mark ran across the fight poster and the weigh-in stage, and sat on the scale itself the day before, when Conor McGregor made weight for his first fight since 2021.

Round one.

The round cards carried SuperFile too. For the co-main event, Paddy Pimblett against Benoît Saint Denis, Indiana Fever guard Sophie Cunningham walked the Octagon as a surprise ring-card holder with the SuperFile “1”. Dana White said he booked her about eight minutes before she went out, and the moment ran across Fox, Sports Illustrated and Bleacher Report.

WWE.

In WWE, the deal brings side-ring-mat placements, presenting-partner designations and custom social and digital content across Premium Live Events including WrestleMania, Money in the Bank, Survivor Series and the Royal Rumble.

Inside the Octagon.

From the White House lawn to UFC 329 in Las Vegas, SuperFile sat on the scale, the round cards and the Octagon itself.

Impact.

The partnership put SuperFile across two major global sports and entertainment properties, reaching audiences through live events, broadcast and digital platforms.

Beyond exposure, it created opportunities for executive networking, investor engagement, strategic introductions and prospective commercial relationships.
