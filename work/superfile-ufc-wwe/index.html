SuperFile × UFC & WWE

A global partnership with TKO that made SuperFile the first Official Secure File Sharing Partner of both UFC and WWE.

Year
2026
Type
Brand Management

TKO and SuperFile announced the partnership on June 9, 2026. Five days later, SuperFile was an Official Partner of UFC FREEDOM 250 at the White House, with Octagon branding and broadcast integrations reaching an estimated one billion households in 210 countries and territories, plus the SuperFile Super Replay, “Keys to Victory” and a one-hour SuperFile Post-Show.

SuperFile went on to present UFC 329, McGregor vs. Holloway 2, at T-Mobile Arena in Las Vegas. In WWE, the deal covers Premium Live Events including WrestleMania, Money in the Bank, Survivor Series and the Royal Rumble, with ring-side placements and custom social content.

Taylor developed and managed the partnership across sponsorship rights, partner assets, brand integration and activation.
