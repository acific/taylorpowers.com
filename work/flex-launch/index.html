FLEX Power Tools Launch

The launch of FLEX’s 24V Stacked Lithium battery: a new kind of pack built to give the whole FLEX lineup more power.

Year
Realm Agency
Type
Account Management

FLEX launched as a brand exclusively with Lowe’s, announced on March 18, 2021, with its first tools on sale that April alongside a “FLEX Face Off” launch event.

Its next step was Stacked Lithium, first shown in November 2021: a pouch-style pack of layered flat cells instead of cylinders, with Power IQ and Therma-Tech+ heat management.

Taylor’s role.

At Realm Agency, Taylor managed the FLEX account through launches like this one, part of the more than 200 product launches Realm supported for EGO, SKIL and FLEX.

Realm Agency.

Product launch · Campaign · Retail

Stacked Lithium.

FLEX claims up to 200% more power, 300% longer battery life and 100% faster charging, in as little as 20 minutes, with every pack cross-compatible with all FLEX 24V tools and chargers. The launch led with a hammer drill, an impact driver and a circular saw, each with a five-year limited warranty.

Power that changes everything.

The campaign spoke straight to the trade: “This is you 200% more,” “Power that changes everything” and #ITSWHATSNEXT, carried through to retail at Lowe’s.
