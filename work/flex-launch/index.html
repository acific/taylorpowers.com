FLEX Power Tools Launch

Turning a century-old German brand into a U.S. contender: FLEX Power Tools’ North American launch, exclusively at Lowe’s.

Year
2021
Type
Brand Launch

At Realm Agency, Taylor helped lead the North American launch of FLEX Power Tools, turning a century-old German brand known for professional polishers and grinders into a major contender in the U.S. cordless market.

With Chervon North America, Realm built and ran the brand and go-to-market strategy behind FLEX’s nationwide retail launch at Lowe’s, from positioning and messaging to product launches, national advertising and retail.

Taylor’s role.

Taylor led agency-side account management, brand strategy, campaign development and integrated marketing with Chervon’s brand and product leadership: market research, competitive positioning, messaging, identity, product positioning and naming, go-to-market planning, creative direction and hands-on oversight of pre-production, production and post.

The omnichannel work covered national TV, product photo and video, CGI and 3D animation, web and e-commerce, paid digital and social, influencers, sponsorship integrations, packaging, merchandising, in-store displays, Amazon and Lowe’s digital channels, trade shows and sales enablement.

Realm Agency.

Product launch · Campaign · Retail

The strategy.

Milwaukee, DeWalt and Makita had spent decades earning tradespeople’s trust, and many pros had invested heavily in their battery platforms. Winning them over took more than a good ad.

FLEX was positioned as the next generation of professional power tools, leaning on German engineering, performance and 24V cordless technology, with performance-led storytelling and demonstrations. The LAFC jersey deal gave it awareness; the wider marketing turned that into product education, retail engagement and demand.

Stacked Lithium.

FLEX claims up to 200% more power, 300% longer battery life and 100% faster charging, in as little as 20 minutes, with every pack cross-compatible with all FLEX 24V tools and chargers. The launch led with a hammer drill, an impact driver and a circular saw, each with a five-year limited warranty.

Power that changes everything.

The campaign spoke straight to the trade: “This is you 200% more,” “Power that changes everything” and #ITSWHATSNEXT, carried through to retail at Lowe’s.

Stacked Lithium, on film.

The launch work carried through to FLEX’s Stacked Lithium battery: more power, longer runtime and faster charging, shown the way pros use their tools.

Impact.

The launch established FLEX as a new competitor in the U.S. professional market, with national recognition and strong early momentum: a reported nearly $29 million in online sales in its first launch quarter in 2021, well ahead of projections.

It also laid a scalable brand and marketing foundation for continued product expansion, retail growth and national advertising.
