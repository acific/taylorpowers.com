SuperFile × Mr. Brainwash Artist Collaboration

The #97 handed to the street artist Mr. Brainwash for Las Vegas, turning a race weekend into a culture moment.

Year
2026
Type
Brand Management

Trackhouse unveiled the livery on March 10, 2026: Mr. Brainwash, the LA-based street artist Thierry Guetta, known for album covers for Madonna, Rick Ross and Kygo, painted Shane van Gisbergen’s No. 97 Chevrolet to celebrate SuperFile’s multi-year, multi-race partnership with the team.

The artwork also marked the launch of SuperFile’s digital ownership platform.

Taylor’s role.

A one-off inside the Trackhouse program Taylor ran as VP of Brand, bringing art into a sport built on speed.

VP of Brand, SuperFile.

Art car · Culture · NASCAR

SuperBrainHouse.

SuperFile called it the “SuperBrainHouse” car. “This is our celebration car,” said Shane Valdez, “launching a multi-year partnership built on speed, art, and innovation.” Launching in Las Vegas was personal: “I wanted it to begin where my story started.”

Las Vegas.

Sprayed neon colours, repeated No. 97 decals and an engine graphic ahead of the rear wheels made it one of the most talked-about schemes of the spring. It debuted at Las Vegas Motor Speedway on March 15. “The car looks epic,” van Gisbergen said, “kind of like the lights on the Vegas Strip.”

Lionel released it as an official die-cast the next day.
