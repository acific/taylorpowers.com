SuperFile × Lefty Out There Artist Collaborations

SuperFile handed Shane van Gisbergen’s #97 to Chicago artist Lefty Out There for NASCAR’s return to Chicagoland.

Year
2026
Type
Brand Management

Trackhouse revealed the one-of-a-kind scheme on July 2, 2026: a Chicago-inspired SuperFile livery designed in part by Francesco Campanella, the Chicago-based visual artist who works as Lefty Out There.

Black and white abstract shapes with neon-yellow accents wrapped the car, with SuperFile on the door and Lefty Out There behind the number.

Taylor’s role.

Part of SuperFile’s run of artist cars inside its Trackhouse Racing partnership, which Taylor led as VP of Brand.

VP of Brand, SuperFile.

Artist collaboration · Livery · Merchandise

Chicagoland.

The car ran in the eero 400 at Chicagoland Speedway, NASCAR’s first Cup race there since 2019, on an oval van Gisbergen had never raced. “It looks really challenging, really bumpy,” he said beforehand. He started 30th and finished 25th.

The artist.

It was Lefty Out There’s second major motorsport project, after a virtual F1 livery with Max Verstappen. The car was also released as Lionel 1:24 and 1:64 die-casts and a T-shirt.
