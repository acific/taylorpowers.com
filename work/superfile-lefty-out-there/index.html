SuperFile × Lefty Out There Artist Collaborations

SuperFile handed Shane van Gisbergen’s #97 to the artist Lefty Out There for Chicagoland.

Year
2026
Type
Brand Management

Lefty Out There, the American artist Francesco Campanella, designed the livery for van Gisbergen’s SuperFile-backed #97 Trackhouse Camaro at the 2026 Chicagoland eero 400. It was his second major motorsport project, after a virtual F1 livery with Max Verstappen.

The collaboration was part of SuperFile’s run of artist cars inside its Trackhouse Racing partnership, which Taylor led as VP of Brand.
