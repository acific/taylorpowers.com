SuperFile Brand Launch

Taking a new kind of security company to market, from its first brand system to some of the biggest stages in sport.

Year
2025 — 2026
Type
Brand Management

SuperFile is a Chicago technology company building what it calls sovereign file control: letting enterprises, governments and creators secure, authenticate, track and revoke access to their content even after it has been shared or downloaded. Its line is “Trust. Control. Simplicity.”

Led by founder and CEO Shane Valdez, the company opened an early-access waitlist where people reserve their own @name while the product is in development, and took the brand straight into sport.

Taylor’s role.

Taylor launched SuperFile’s brand, building and leading a 25+ person department across brand, strategy, creative, partnerships and production, and aligning marketing, product and sales on go-to-market.

On social, Taylor launched and scaled SuperFile’s Instagram to more than 150,000 followers in eight months, averaging 18,000+ new followers a month.

VP of Brand, SuperFile.

Brand development · Integrated marketing · Content · Social

From zero to the world stage.

In 2026 the brand debuted at the Daytona 500, became title sponsor of a MotoGP team, went inside the Octagon at UFC FREEDOM 250 at the White House, signed on for WWE’s Premium Live Events and went onto the front of LAFC’s training kit.

Each partnership carried the same black and neon-yellow identity, from a race car’s hood to a fighter’s glove.

The film.

SuperFile’s brand film, released by Valdez in October 2024, set out the idea before the product: “SuperFile isn’t just a tool — it’s a vision. It’s for creators.”
