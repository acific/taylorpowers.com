SKIL Power Tools

Making a 100-year-old American brand matter to a new generation of DIYers: the Gas Zombies campaign and SKIL’s 2024 brand refresh.

Year
2022 — 2024
Type
Brand Campaign

At Realm Agency, Taylor helped lead the modernisation of SKIL Power Tools, a 100-year-old American brand whose legacy goes back to the invention of the circular saw in 1924.

From the viral Gas Zombies campaign to a full brand refresh in 2024, the work combined strategic positioning, original storytelling and omnichannel execution to make an established brand relevant to today’s consumers.

Taylor’s role.

Taylor co-developed the Gas Zombies concept and led its creative development alongside Realm CEO Shane Valdez and the SKIL team, from storyboards through production and delivery.

Across the account she led management, brand strategy, campaign development and integrated marketing with Chervon’s brand leadership: research, insights, positioning, messaging, visual identity, creative direction and hands-on production, plus retail and e-commerce across Lowe’s, Ace Hardware and Amazon.

Realm Agency.

National TV · Campaign · Product launch

The strategy.

A century of recognition wasn’t enough to win modern consumers. The challenge was to build on the legacy while making SKIL more distinctive and approachable for today’s DIY audience, from two directions: campaigns people would notice, and a stronger brand foundation to keep them.

Gas Zombies used humour and a cinematic look to challenge reliance on gas-powered equipment, turning an everyday product benefit into a story people remembered.

The spot.

It plays for laughs: a couple shares their SKIL tools with their zombie neighbors, while one stubborn holdout tears his arm off trying to start his gas equipment. It closes on SKIL’s tagline, “What Powers You?”, with the range available at Lowe’s.

The reach.

SKIL’s own 30-second upload had passed 18.9 million views on YouTube by November 2026, pointing viewers to a single call to action: ditch the gas.

The 2024 refresh.

Building on the campaign work, Realm led a comprehensive brand refresh in 2024, using market research, competitive analysis and audience insights to evolve SKIL’s identity, messaging and marketing for the modern DIY consumer.

Impact.

The work brought fresh creative energy to a century-old name. Gas Zombies drew nearly 19 million views on its original YouTube release alone, and the 2024 refresh set a new strategic and creative foundation connecting SKIL’s history of innovation with a new generation.
