SKIL Gas Zombies Campaign

“Don’t Be a Gas Zombie”: SKIL’s national TV spot for its PWRCORE 40 cordless outdoor range.

Year
2022
Type
Account Management

SKIL’s spot for PWRCORE 40, first published in March 2022, pitched its brushless 40V string trimmer, leaf blower, self-propelled mower and chainsaw as the way out of a world controlled by gas.

SKIL carried the same idea across its pages: “Ditch the gas, keep the power.”

Taylor’s role.

Part of Realm Agency’s work across Chervon’s brands, where Taylor led integrated campaigns from concept through delivery, coordinating creative teams, production, post-production and external partners.

Realm Agency.

National TV · Campaign · Product launch

The spot.

It plays for laughs: a couple shares their SKIL tools with their zombie neighbors, while one stubborn holdout tears his arm off trying to start his gas equipment. It closes on SKIL’s tagline, “What Powers You?”, with the range available at Lowe’s.

The reach.

SKIL’s own 30-second upload had passed 18.9 million views on YouTube by November 2026, pointing viewers to a single call to action: ditch the gas.
