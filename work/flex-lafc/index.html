FLEX Power Tools × LAFC

FLEX Power Tools on the front of LAFC’s jersey for five years: the sports platform behind a new power tool brand’s national launch.

Year
2021 — 2023
Type
Account Management

At Realm Agency, Taylor helped lead the launch and activation of FLEX Power Tools’ five-year, front-of-jersey partnership with Los Angeles Football Club, supporting the brand’s introduction to the U.S. professional power tool market.

Timed to FLEX’s nationwide retail launch at Lowe’s, the partnership became a cornerstone of its marketing, combining national broadcast exposure, integrated advertising, fan engagement, retail promotions and experiential activations.

Taylor’s role.

Taylor helped lead agency-side strategy, planning and activation with FLEX leadership, LAFC, creative teams and production partners: brand positioning, campaign development, sponsorship integrations, creative direction, branded content and hands-on production across digital, social, broadcast and retail.

The program also included match-day activations, VIP and customer hospitality, fan experiences, co-branded merchandise and community work, including FLEX’s Power to Empower program.

Account Director → Senior Account Director, Realm Agency.

Jersey sponsorship · Campaign development · Partner relations · Experiential

The strategy.

FLEX was entering a market dominated by established professional brands. The challenge: build immediate awareness, earn credibility with tradespeople and position FLEX as a serious contender.

Realm identified LAFC for the fit between the club’s cultural identity, its passionate fanbase and FLEX’s audience of contractors and tradespeople. Rather than a logo placement, it became an integrated platform across jerseys, stadium branding, broadcast, digital, retail and community.

Welcome to the Club.

LAFC announced the partnership with “Welcome to the Club FLEX Power Tools.” Two days later the club unveiled its Heart of Gold Community Kit with FLEX across the middle, gifting jerseys to essential workers at the launch.

Three seasons on the front.

FLEX stayed across the chest through the 2022 “5 Years Strong” primary kit and the 2023 “Smokescreen” away kit, both revealed at kit launches presented by FLEX Power Tools.

Match days.

On the pitch and in the stands, FLEX was part of the club: from the 2022 title run to fan events, player meet-ups and community days.

Impact.

The partnership established FLEX’s national presence, connecting the brand with a passionate sports audience as it entered the U.S. professional market.

Across five years it generated a reported 60 billion+ media impressions, and FLEX hit its sales targets within the first six months of launch.
