FLEX Power Tools × LAFC

FLEX Power Tools on the front of Los Angeles FC’s jersey: a multi-year deal that made a new tool brand part of one of MLS’s loudest clubs.

Year
2021 — 2023
Type
Account Management

LAFC announced FLEX as its Official Jersey Sponsor on March 26, 2021, putting the brand on every Club, Community and specialty jersey. FLEX also became the club’s Official Power Tool Partner and a Golden Boot Club Partner, with exclusive content, media, signage and a co-branded retail promotion, timed to FLEX’s 24V line launching nationwide.

At Realm Agency, Taylor worked across the sponsorship from campaign development and partner relations to experiential activations, including the club’s 2022 “5 Years Strong” kit with FLEX across the chest.

The program reached more than 60 billion impressions.
