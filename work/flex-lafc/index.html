FLEX Power Tools × LAFC

FLEX Power Tools on the front of Los Angeles FC’s jersey: a multi-year deal that launched a new tool brand with one of MLS’s loudest clubs.

Year
2021 — 2023
Type
Account Management

On March 26, 2021, LAFC named FLEX its Official Jersey Sponsor, replacing YouTube TV and putting FLEX on every Club, Community and specialty jersey. It was the first sports partnership for FLEX’s parent company, Chervon North America.

The deal made FLEX the club’s Official Power Tool Partner and a member of its top Golden Boot Club tier, with exclusive content, media, signage and a co-branded retail promotion, timed to FLEX’s launch at Lowe’s that spring.

Taylor’s role.

At Realm Agency, Taylor worked across the sponsorship from campaign development and partner relations to experiential activations. The program reached more than 60 billion impressions.

Account Director → Senior Account Director, Realm Agency.

Jersey sponsorship · Campaign development · Partner relations · Experiential

Welcome to the Club.

LAFC announced the partnership with “Welcome to the Club FLEX Power Tools.” Two days later the club unveiled its Heart of Gold Community Kit with FLEX across the middle, gifting jerseys to essential workers at the launch.

Three seasons on the front.

FLEX stayed across the chest through the 2022 “5 Years Strong” primary kit and the 2023 “Smokescreen” away kit, both revealed at kit launches presented by FLEX Power Tools.
