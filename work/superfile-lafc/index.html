SuperFile × LAFC

SuperFile on the front of LAFC’s training kit, launched with the club’s own announcement film.

Year
2026
Type
Brand Management

Early in 2026 SuperFile became a Golden Boot Club partner of Los Angeles FC, the club’s top partnership tier, and the front-of-training-kit sponsor, putting the brand on the LAFC Training Kit the squad wears every day.

LAFC gave fans a first look on April 4: “Final touches before kickoff. First look in the SuperFile training tops.”

Taylor’s role.

Taylor developed and managed the partnership as part of SuperFile’s sports program.

VP of Brand, SuperFile.

Sports partnership · Training kit · Brand integration · Content

The kit.

LAFC describes the kit as designed for the modern athlete and built for the daily grind, bridging high-performance technology and the club’s Black & Gold look. The club introduced it with a SuperFile announcement film shot with the players.

Matchday.

The tops went straight into pre-match warm-ups at BMO Stadium, in white for the squad and green for the goalkeepers.

A force for good.

To mark the start of the partnership, SuperFile donated $50,000 to the LAFC Foundation to support youth soccer and grow the game across Los Angeles.
