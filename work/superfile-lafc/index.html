SuperFile × LAFC

SuperFile on LAFC’s training kit and in its Golden Boot Club: a partnership with one of MLS’s most recognisable clubs, built for visibility, relationships and community.

Year
2026
Type
Brand Management

As VP of Brand at SuperFile, Taylor led the brand-side strategy and activation of SuperFile’s partnership with Los Angeles Football Club, making SuperFile an official training kit sponsor and Golden Boot Club partner.

The partnership aligned SuperFile with one of Major League Soccer’s most recognisable clubs, creating national brand exposure, fan engagement, executive hospitality and business development.

Taylor’s role.

Taylor led the brand-side strategy, planning and execution: sponsorship integration, marketing, creative direction, branded content and hands-on production.

Her remit also covered match-day activations, VIP and executive hospitality, partner events, guest experiences, co-branded merchandise, gifting, food and beverage, and coordination with LAFC’s partnership and marketing teams, including a SuperFile-presented MLS match, curated executive networking and community programs.

VP of Brand, SuperFile.

Sports partnership · Training kit · Brand integration · Content

The strategy.

As SuperFile prepared for market, it wanted to stand beside an organisation known for sporting excellence, cultural relevance and a strong business community.

Taylor had worked with LAFC before, on FLEX Power Tools’ front-of-jersey partnership, and knew the club could deliver value beyond exposure. The training kit gave consistent visibility through training sessions, club media and digital content; with match-day activations and LAFC’s corporate network, it became a platform for recognition, credibility and relationships.

The kit.

LAFC describes the kit as designed for the modern athlete and built for the daily grind, bridging high-performance technology and the club’s Black & Gold look. The club introduced it with a SuperFile announcement film shot with the players.

Matchday.

The tops went straight into pre-match warm-ups at BMO Stadium, in white for the squad and green for the goalkeepers.

A force for good.

To mark the start of the partnership, SuperFile donated $50,000 to the LAFC Foundation to support youth soccer and grow the game across Los Angeles.

Impact.

The partnership established SuperFile in Major League Soccer, with sustained visibility and opportunities for executive relationships, strategic introductions and prospective business partnerships.

It also showed SuperFile’s commitment to community, through a $50,000 contribution to the LAFC Foundation supporting access to youth soccer across Los Angeles.
