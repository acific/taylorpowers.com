EGO announced its next-generation Z6 riders on March 17, 2026, under the line “All power. Zero compromise.” They are rated up to 27 HP gas-equivalent and up to three acres on a single charge, with an 80 mm wider track, 30% more peak torque than the first Z6 and IPX5 weather resistance.

“All power, zero compromise,” said Tim Baker, EGO’s vice president of product.
