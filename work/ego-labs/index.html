EGO Labs 2.0

Launch work for EGO’s next generation of Z6 zero-turn riding mowers: all the power of gas, with no exhaust and no oil changes.

Year
Realm Agency
Type
Account Management / Production

EGO introduced its next generation of Z6 zero-turn riding mowers with the line “All power. Zero compromise.” The range runs from the 42-inch Z6 to the 52-inch ZT5216L, rated up to 27 HP gas-equivalent and up to three acres on a single charge, plus a 42-inch model with E-Steer that drives like a car and the T6 lawn tractor.

Peak Power combines up to six of EGO’s 56V batteries, the same ones that run more than 100 EGO tools, and a color display connects to the EGO Connect app for blade speed, driving mode and mowing history. EGO calls itself the number one rated brand in cordless outdoor power, with more than 250,000 five-star reviews.

At Realm Agency, Taylor led account management and production for EGO across product launches and campaign content.
