SuperFile Trackhouse MotoGP

The first title partner in Trackhouse MotoGP history: SuperFile took its Trackhouse relationship from American motorsport to a world championship on five continents.

Year
2026
Type
Brand Management

Building on the NASCAR partnership, SuperFile expanded its relationship with Trackhouse Racing into the MotoGP World Championship, becoming the first-ever title partner of the Trackhouse MotoGP Team.

The multi-year naming-rights agreement created the SuperFile Trackhouse MotoGP Team, extending the brand from American motorsport to a global championship spanning five continents.

Taylor’s role.

As VP of Brand, Taylor led SuperFile’s brand-side strategy and activation, overseeing the partnership’s creative direction, brand integration, marketing and hands-on production.

That covered international race-weekend activations, VIP and executive hospitality, guest experiences, branded merchandise, promotional swag and gifting, partner communications and cross-functional production logistics, working with Trackhouse and outside partners to turn the title sponsorship into one global brand presence.

VP of Brand, SuperFile.

Title partnership · Content · Hospitality

The strategy.

Working with Trackhouse had shown the value of a partner that shared SuperFile’s ambition to challenge convention. MotoGP was the natural next step: building on an established relationship while introducing SuperFile to international audiences.

The title partnership went deeper than logos, with SuperFile across the team’s identity, bikes, rider apparel and merchandise, and a platform for international business development, executive networking and race-weekend hospitality.

Assen.

At the Dutch TT in June the SuperFile bikes finished first and second twice. Raúl Fernández won the Sprint from fourth on the grid with Ai Ogura second; the next day Ogura took his first MotoGP win with Fernández second.

Ogura became the first Japanese premier-class winner since 2004, and motogp.com noted that a double one-two in Sprint and Grand Prix had only been done before by the Ducati Lenovo Team.

A summer on the podium.

Ogura took pole at Brno and finished second. At the Sachsenring the team scored a second double podium in a row, Ogura second and Fernández third. At Silverstone in August, Fernández led from lights to flag for his second MotoGP win.

Impact.

The expansion strengthened SuperFile’s relationship with Trackhouse and established the brand in one of the world’s premier motorsport championships.

Turning an existing partnership into a global title sponsorship gave SuperFile a broader platform for international awareness, strategic relationships and long-term commercial opportunities.
