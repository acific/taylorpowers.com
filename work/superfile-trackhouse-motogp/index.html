SuperFile Trackhouse MotoGP

From logos on the fairing to the name on the team: SuperFile became the first title sponsor of Trackhouse’s MotoGP team.

Year
2026
Type
Brand Management

On June 5, 2026, SuperFile became title partner of Trackhouse’s MotoGP team, the last team on the grid without one, in what both sides called a long-term strategic commitment. The team races as the SuperFile Trackhouse MotoGP Team.

The black-and-yellow SuperFile livery runs for the rest of the season, starting at the Hungarian Grand Prix at Balaton Park, with riders Raúl Fernández and Ai Ogura, after a first SuperFile livery at the Spanish Grand Prix.

Building on the NASCAR program, Taylor ran the partnership across rights, assets, paddock content and hospitality at each round.
