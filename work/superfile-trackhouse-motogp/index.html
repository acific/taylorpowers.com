SuperFile Trackhouse MotoGP

From logos on the fairing to the name on the team: SuperFile became the first title sponsor of Trackhouse’s MotoGP team, and the bikes started winning.

Year
2026
Type
Brand Management

SuperFile first appeared on the Trackhouse Aprilias at the Spanish Grand Prix at Jerez in April 2026, the team’s first crossover between its NASCAR and MotoGP programs.

On June 5 it became the team’s title sponsor, the first since Trackhouse entered MotoGP in 2024, with the black-and-yellow livery running from the Hungarian Grand Prix at Balaton Park. “What excites me most isn’t seeing the SuperFile name on the bike,” said Shane Valdez. “It’s who we’re building with.”

Taylor’s role.

Building on the NASCAR program, Taylor ran the partnership across rights, assets, paddock content and hospitality at each round.

VP of Brand, SuperFile.

Title partnership · Content · Hospitality

Assen.

At the Dutch TT in June the SuperFile bikes finished first and second twice. Raúl Fernández won the Sprint from fourth on the grid with Ai Ogura second; the next day Ogura took his first MotoGP win with Fernández second.

Ogura became the first Japanese premier-class winner since 2004, and motogp.com noted that a double one-two in Sprint and Grand Prix had only been done before by the Ducati Lenovo Team.

A summer on the podium.

Ogura took pole at Brno and finished second. At the Sachsenring the team scored a second double podium in a row, Ogura second and Fernández third. At Silverstone in August, Fernández led from lights to flag for his second MotoGP win.
