Digital Domain

Visual effects coordination for Digital Domain’s Digital Human Group, the team behind some of the most convincing digital people on screen.

Year
2020 — 2021
Type
VFX Coordinator

Digital Domain’s Digital Human Group pioneers digital and virtual humans for every medium, building on the studio’s work on Thanos in Avengers: Infinity War and The Curious Case of Benjamin Button.

Its tools include Masquerade facial capture, the Charlatan face-replacement system, machine-learning clothing and real-time digital-human rendering, used across film, live events, games and mobile.

Taylor’s role.

Taylor coordinated visual effects production for the group, managing international workflows, artist schedules and deliverables.

VFX Coordinator, Digital Domain.

VFX production · Digital humans · Machine learning

DigiDoug.

At TED in 2019, Doug Roble, Digital Domain’s head of software R&D, gave a talk alongside DigiDoug, a real-time digital double driven live from a single camera.

Masquerade and Charlatan.

In September 2020 the group released Masquerade 2.0, rebuilt facial-capture technology from the Thanos work that uses machine learning to run ten times faster and cut artist time by 95%.

Charlatan, its machine-learning face-replacement system, aged David Beckham by about 30 years for Malaria No More’s “Malaria Must Die” campaign.

Vince Lombardi.

In February 2021 the group worked with the NFL and 72andSunny on a digital Vince Lombardi for Super Bowl LV, made with his estate’s blessing. Masquerade 2.0 won a Lumière Award, and the NFL spot won a Silver Telly and a Silver Clio.
