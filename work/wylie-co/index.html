Wylie Co.

Visual effects production at Wylie Co., the studio behind postvis and final effects on Dune, including the Fremen’s blue eyes.

Year
2017 — 2020
Type
VFX Coordinator

Wylie Co. is a boutique visual effects studio in Culver City and Montreal, founded in 2015 as Rebellion VFX. Its credits run from Everest and Terminator: Dark Fate to Beastie Boys Story, The Stand and Dune.

The studio works across the whole pipeline: previsualization, postvis that lets editors cut with temporary effects, and final shots.

Taylor’s role.

As VFX Coordinator, Taylor managed production workflows across the studio’s film and television slate, including Dune: Part One, Terminator: Dark Fate and Marvel productions.

Dune.

On Dune (2021), Wylie completed around 450 postvis shots and 950 visual effects shots. DNEG led the film’s effects, with Wylie Co. and Rodeo FX contributing.

To help Denis Villeneuve plan the scale of Arrakis, Wylie built a Vislab and an iOS app, Wylie Cam, that let him frame wide shots from an iPad.

The Eyes of Ibad.

Wylie delivered the Fremen’s blue “Eyes of Ibad”: nearly 450 eye shots, each tracked and composited so the glow sat naturally in every frame.

The Oscar.

Dune won Best Visual Effects at the 94th Academy Awards, and the BAFTA and VES awards for its effects.
