SuperFile × Trackhouse Racing NASCAR

SuperFile’s first NASCAR partnership arrived on the sport’s biggest stage: Shane van Gisbergen’s black-and-neon #97 Chevrolet at the 2026 Daytona 500.

Year
2026
Type
Brand Management

On February 6, 2026, Trackhouse Racing announced SuperFile as the sponsor of Shane van Gisbergen’s No. 97 Chevrolet for the 68th Daytona 500. It was the Chicago company’s first step into NASCAR, introducing a brand built on what it calls sovereign file control: content that stays “trackable, unhackable, takebackable.”

“There is no better place than Daytona to introduce SuperFile to NASCAR for the first time,” van Gisbergen said. Trackhouse owner Justin Marks added that SuperFile “has a very unique position in the tech space.”

Taylor’s role.

Taylor oversaw the sponsorship rights, partner assets and brand integration, and built the event operation behind race-weekend hospitality for investors, clients and customers, from logistics and vendors to on-site execution.

Daytona.

The black and neon-yellow No. 97 started 13th in the Daytona 500 and led laps 101 to 103 before it was caught in the late Stage 2 crash. After repairs it finished 30th, one lap down.

The race set the look for SuperFile’s season: a dark car with the SuperFile mark across the hood.

A season of liveries.

The deal grew into a multi-year, multi-race partnership, and the No. 97 changed its look through the year: a Mr. Brainwash art car for Las Vegas in March, a “SuperFile Salutes” digital-camo military tribute for the Coca-Cola 600 on Memorial Day weekend, and a Chicago-inspired livery by the artist Lefty Out There at Chicagoland in July.

SuperFile was also announced as primary sponsor of Connor Zilisch’s No. 88 for New Hampshire in August, and several of the cars became official Lionel die-casts.

Watkins Glen.

On May 10, van Gisbergen won at Watkins Glen from pole, leading 74 of 100 laps and finishing more than seven seconds clear for his seventh Cup Series victory.

“Yeah, this SuperFile Chevy was great,” he said afterwards.
