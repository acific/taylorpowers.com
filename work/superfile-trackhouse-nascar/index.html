SuperFile × Trackhouse Racing NASCAR

SuperFile’s first NASCAR partnership arrived on the sport’s biggest stage: the black-and-neon #97 Chevrolet Camaro for Shane van Gisbergen at the 2026 Daytona 500.

Year
2026
Type
Brand Management

Trackhouse Racing announced the partnership on February 6, 2026, nine days before the Daytona 500, putting the cybersecurity file-management platform on Shane van Gisbergen’s #97 Chevrolet in a black and neon livery.

Taylor oversaw the sponsorship rights, partner assets and brand integration, and built the event operation behind race-weekend hospitality for investors, clients and customers, from logistics and vendors to on-site execution.

The program grew into a multi-year, multi-race partnership: a Mr. Brainwash art car in Las Vegas, an artist livery by Lefty Out There at Chicagoland, and title sponsorship of Trackhouse’s MotoGP team.
