Shane van Gisbergen put the SuperFile Chevrolet on pole for Go Bowling at The Glen, led 74 of the 100 laps and won by more than seven seconds.

MotoGP — June & August 2026

One-two at Assen. Lights to flag at Silverstone.

1–2Sprint and Grand Prix, Dutch TT

SuperFile Trackhouse MotoGP Team

2
Grand Prix wins in SuperFile colours

At the Dutch TT the SuperFile Aprilias finished first and second twice in one weekend. Raúl Fernández won the Sprint; the next day Ai Ogura took his first MotoGP win, the first for a Japanese rider in the premier class since 2004.

In August, Fernández led every lap at Silverstone for his second Grand Prix win.

MLS Cup — November 5, 2022

Champions, with FLEX on the front.

128′Bale’s equaliser, the latest goal in MLS history

FLEX Power Tools × LAFC

3–0
On penalties after a 3–3 draw

LAFC won its first MLS Cup in FLEX’s second season on the front of the shirt. Gareth Bale’s header in the 128th minute forced a shootout, and LAFC beat the Philadelphia Union 3–0 on penalties.

With the Supporters’ Shield already won, it completed the club’s first double.

The ring

128 black diamonds.

LAFC presented its championship rings, made with Baron, on March 4, 2023. The top carries 128 black diamonds for Bale’s 128th-minute goal, set in a black obsidian finish that was a first in major league sports.
