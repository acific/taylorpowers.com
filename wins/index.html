NASCAR Cup Series · May 10, 2026

Victory lane at Watkins Glen.

Shane van Gisbergen won Go Bowling at The Glen from pole in the SuperFile Chevrolet, leading 74 of 100 laps and finishing more than seven seconds clear for his seventh Cup Series win.

Taylor, who ran the SuperFile partnership, was in victory lane with the team.

MotoGP · June and August 2026

One-two at Assen. A win at Silverstone.

At the Dutch TT the SuperFile Aprilias finished first and second in both the Sprint and the Grand Prix: Raúl Fernández won the Sprint, and Ai Ogura took his first MotoGP win, the first for a Japanese rider in the premier class since 2004.

In August Fernández led from lights to flag at Silverstone for his second MotoGP win.

MLS Cup · November 5, 2022

Champions, with FLEX on the front.

LAFC won its first MLS Cup in FLEX’s second season on the front of the shirt, beating the Philadelphia Union 3–0 on penalties after a 3–3 draw. Gareth Bale’s header in the 128th minute, the latest goal in MLS history, forced the shootout.

With the Supporters’ Shield already won, it completed LAFC’s first double.
